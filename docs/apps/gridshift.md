# Gridshift

> "A reimagining of the macOS music creation experience"



### 📋 The Vitals
- **Category:** DAW
- **Platform:** macOS Beta, iOS on Testflight
- **Pricing:** Free in beta
- **My Rating:** 6/10

### ✅ What It Does Well
It's very well designed, in my humble opinion. It seems quite simple to use, though I couldn't tell you as I don't make music myself.

### ❌ What It Doesn't
It is not like Logic Pro. I think it's more like FL Studio, but I'm not sure because I haven't used FL Studio. 

### 🏁 Final Verdict
If you have a MacBook or a Mac and some free time, go get it, download it, try it out [from right here](https://gridshift.studio). I don't see why you shouldn't.
