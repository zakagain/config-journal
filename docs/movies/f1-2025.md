# F1 (2025) | Review

> "Sunday, Daytona."

### 📋 The Vitals
*   **Director:** Joseph Kosinski
*   **Genre:** Action, Drama
*   **My Rating:** 9/10


### 📖 The Premise
Racing legend Sonny Hayes is coaxed out of retirement to lead a struggling Formula 1 team—and mentor a young hotshot driver—while chasing one more chance at glory.

### ⚙️ My Opinion
I thought this movie was incredibly directed, the cinematography definitely was the key player in this decision. When it's an Apple original film, you can really feel the 'Pursuit Of Excellence' in action.

### 🏁 Final Verdict
You've seen my rating. I would definitely recommend you go watch it, even if you're not into cars.
