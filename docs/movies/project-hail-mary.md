> "Both on our distinct individual separate... separate."



### 📋 The Vitals
*   **Director:** Phil Lord, Christopher Miller
*   **Genre:** Sci-Fi, Drama, Comedy
*   **My Rating:** 10/10


### 📖 The Premise
Science teacher Ryland Grace wakes up on a spaceship light years from home with no recollection of who he is or how he got there. As his memory returns, he begins to uncover his mission: solve the riddle of the mysterious substance causing the sun to die out. He must call on his scientific knowledge and unorthodox ideas to save everything on Earth from extinction.


### ⚙️ My Opinion
I thought it was a genuinely amazing movie and I've heard a lot of people say that it tops Interstellar, however, at first I was sceptical, but upon watching the movie and further reconsideration, it comes with great sadness to say that: this is now my favourite movie. It was more humorous than Interstellar, it was a bit shorter and easier to get into and And I just think the main actor was that little bit better. 


### 🏁 Final Verdict
It's an absolute masterpiece. I don't think I'll be finding anything to top it anytime soon.
